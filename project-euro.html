Teams
& Squads

Challenge

In Taiwan, basketball and baseball own the sports conversation. The Euros rarely break through.

But there's a small, loyal group of football fans here who follow every match, and the world around it. Since the war in Ukraine began in 2022, FIFA and UEFA have banned Russia's national team and clubs from their competitions, and football has become tangled up with world events in a way it hadn't been for decades. This book needed to speak to those fans, and give them something they couldn't get from a screen.

You'll never
walk alone

The whole book grows out of one line, the slogan Ukrainians carried through the war: "You'll never walk alone." It connects a handful of fans in Taiwan with people living under fire on the other side of the world. However small the crowd, football can still bring it together.

A cover you
play with

At a time when print is losing ground, a physical book has to offer what a screen can't: paper, ink and your own hands. So I made the cover something you open up.

The outer cover is a sheet of stickers, with illustrated star players from all 24 teams. Peel it away and the real cover underneath is an empty football pitch. Readers place their favorite players wherever they want and build their own lineup, and their own story.

Inside
the book

Each team gets a player feature and a squad page. On the features, the player's name wraps around his body like a ribbon, set against a wash of national colors. The squad pages pack the numbers fans want into one spread: stars, lineups, kits and quick facts.

I designed everything in the book myself, from the art direction and layout to the illustrations and infographics.

Match
schedule

The back of the poster is a full tournament schedule, set in Taiwan time so fans know exactly when to stay up. A calendar shows every match day at a glance, a grid maps each game to its stadium, and group tables follow the bracket all the way to the final.

Inside the book, a map of Germany links all ten host cities to the teams playing there.

Credits

Printed on 120P woodfree and 126P matte coated paper, and released on June 14, 2024, the opening day of the tournament.
