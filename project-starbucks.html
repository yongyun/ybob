Motion design Illustration Campaign

Concept

For Starbucks' 2022 Moon Festival campaign, the design started from the decorative windows found in traditional Taiwanese architecture.

Those familiar window frames became a way to look back at something warmer: the memories and the human warmth that so many people in Taiwan connect with home and with the festival itself.

Through
the window

Each short film looks through a different window shape onto a small Moon Festival moment at home.

Outcome

The campaign received a warm response from local audiences, and the collaboration continued with a new design project in 2023.
